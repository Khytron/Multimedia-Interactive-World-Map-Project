About This Project

The Interactive World Map: Culture & History is an educational tool designed to foster spatial-historical literacy across six key regions:

  • North America - Discover Maya, Aztec, and Indigenous cultures
  • South America - Explore the Inca Empire and Amazon heritage
  • Europe - Journey through the Renaissance and Classical era
  • Africa - Learn about ancient Egypt and pre-colonial empires
  • Asia - Explore the Silk Road, ancient dynasties, and the Islamic Golden Age
  • Oceania - Understand Aboriginal and Pacific Islander history

Use the timeline slider to travel through time from 3000 BC to the present day. Click on cultural markers to explore detailed information about historical events, figures, and traditions.
